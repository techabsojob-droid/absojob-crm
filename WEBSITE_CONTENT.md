## Three Portals, One Platform

### 🛡️ Super Admin Portal — Full Control
Command center for founders and staffing business owners.

- **Dashboard & analytics** — live KPIs: active clients, open positions, pipeline health, revenue, receivables
- **Client management** — onboard corporate clients, track agreements and billing contacts
- **Requisition approvals** — review and approve job demands raised by your team before sourcing begins
- **Candidate database** — searchable talent pool with blacklist controls and rating system
- **Team management** — roles, permissions, and account status for recruiters, managers, and agents
- **Finance module** — placement commissions, referral incentives, payout approvals, and invoice tracking
- **Reports & insights** — recruiter leaderboards, source effectiveness, client revenue, monthly placement trends
- **Announcements & audit log** — broadcast company-wide updates; every critical action is recorded

### 🎯 Talent Acquisition Portal — Built for Recruiters
A focused workspace for TA managers and recruiters to close positions faster.

- **My Desk dashboard** — personal requisitions, pipeline snapshot, today's interviews, pending tasks
- **Requisition board** — all client demands with priority flags, fill-rate progress, and days-open tracking
- **Visual sourcing pipeline** — move candidates across stages: Sourced → Screening → Interviews → Tech/Client/HR rounds → Offer → Joined
- **Candidate search** — instantly find profiles by skill, company, experience, or expected CTC
- **Interview scheduling** — schedule rounds, share meeting links, capture structured feedback and scores
- **Task manager** — never miss a follow-up with due-date tracking and priorities

### 🤝 Agent & Employee Portal — Referrals That Reward
Turn your network — channel agents and employees — into a scalable sourcing engine.

- **Refer in seconds** — submit candidates against live openings directly from the portal
- **Live referral status** — track each referral through review, shortlist, and placement
- **Incentive ledger** — complete transparency: credits earned, payouts approved, amounts paid
- **Attendance check-in/out** — daily punch-in with monthly logs, streaks, and hours worked
- **Leave management** — apply for leave, view balances, track approvals
- **Profile & earnings** — lifetime incentives and engagement summary at a glance

## How It Works

1. **Onboard a client** → Super Admin adds the company and commercial terms
2. **Raise & approve requisitions** → Recruiters log open positions; admins approve them for sourcing
3. **Source & screen** → Recruiters and referral partners push candidates into the pipeline
4. **Interview & select** → Schedule rounds, collect feedback, advance the best fits
5. **Place & pay** → Candidate joins, commission is billed to the client, referral incentives flow to partners automatically through the ledger

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is AbsoJob suitable for small teams?**
Yes. Start with a single admin and a couple of recruiters — the workflows scale as you add clients and partners.

**How do referral incentives work?**
Every referral is tied to a ledger entry. When a referred candidate joins and completes onboarding, the incentive is credited, approved by finance, and marked paid — visible to the agent in real time.

**Can I control who sees what?**
Role-based access control separates Super Admin, Talent Acquisition, and Partner data by default. Users only see what their role permits.

**Does it support multiple clients under one agency?**
Yes — AbsoJob is multi-client by design. Each client has its own jobs, contacts, and revenue reporting.

**Is candidate data safe?**
Blacklisting, audit logging, and role-scoped access ensure candidate and client data stays protected and accountable.
